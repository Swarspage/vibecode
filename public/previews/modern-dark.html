Ambient Depth

Instead of relying on harsh contrast, the system creates depth through extremely subtle gradients, large 150px blurred ambient blobs, and a 2% opacity grid overlay that grounds the floating elements.

⌘

Precision Shadows

Elements don't just cast one shadow. They use a 4-layer stack to simulate realistic elevated lighting and rim-highlights.

⚡

Cinematic Motion

Animations are short (200-300ms) but use steep `expo-out` bezier curves so they feel incredibly snappy and responsive to user input.
