Design System

Nothing
but type.

Minimalist Monochrome strips the interface to its absolute core. Black on white. Serif on silence. When every decision is a constraint, every constraint becomes an expression.

Apply This System → View all systems

Principles

  • Zero radius, always

    Every corner is 90 degrees. Sharp geometry is non-negotiable.

  • No accent colors. Ever.

    Black IS the accent. Inversion replaces color for emphasis.

  • Typography as structure

    Headlines don't inform — they dominate. Scale is the hierarchy.

Typography

Playfair Display at full volume

Headlines at 9xl. Words that function as graphic elements. The serif is not decoration — it is the design.

 Depth without shadows

Inversion creates emphasis

Color inversion — black to white, white to black — replaces elevation. No shadows, no gradients. Depth through contrast alone.

 Motion

Instant. Binary. Purposeful.

100ms transitions maximum. States are on or off. Nothing eases in — it arrives.
