Manifesto Design System

Form
follows
function.

The Bauhaus design system reduces every interface to its geometric essence. Circles, squares, and triangles. Primary colors, thick borders, and hard shadows. Nothing is decoration. Everything is structure.

Principles
3 elements
01 — Geometry

Every shape is intentional

Circles, squares, and triangles are the only decorative vocabulary. Nothing is arbitrary. Each form serves a structural purpose.

 02 — Color

Three primaries. No exceptions.

Red, Blue, and Yellow are not accents — they are the palette. Used in full-section blocks, not sprinkled as highlights.

 03 — Borders

Thick lines define the grid

2px and 4px solid black borders aren't decoration — they are the structure. Hard shadows replace elevation. Nothing floats.
