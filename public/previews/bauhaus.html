Principles

3 Elements
I.

Geometry

Everything is built from primitives. Circles are fully rounded, everything else has zero border-radius.

II.

Color Block

Strict adherence to pure primary colors: Red (#D02020), Blue (#1040C0), and Yellow (#F0C020).

III.

Hard Shadows

Solid black offsets (4px or 8px) with thick 4px borders. Hover states remove shadows and translate elements.
