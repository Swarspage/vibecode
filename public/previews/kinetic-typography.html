01

Viewport Scale Type

Headlines use responsive clamp() functions to scale massively, filling empty space and treating typography as the primary architectural element of the page.

02

Zero Radius

Soft corners dampen energy. By enforcing a strict 0px border-radius across all components, the design feels aggressive, sharp, and decisively modern.

03

Hard Inversions

Hover states don't rely on subtle opacity shifts. They execute complete color inversions (black to acid yellow, or black to white) for maximum tactile feedback.

Infinite Marquees

Moving text replaces static borders and dividers, keeping the eye constantly engaged and moving down the page.

Brutal Borders

Consistent 2px borders establish rigid grid structures, preventing the massive typography from turning into chaotic noise.

High Contrast

Rich black (#09090B) and off-white (#FAFAFA) provide the ultimate foundation for legibility at extreme scales.

Acid Accents

Yellow is used sparingly as a highlight color, drawing attention instantly to CTA buttons and key headline phrases.
