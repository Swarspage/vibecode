The intersection of brutalism and editorial elegance.

By stripping away decorative UI elements like shadows and rounded corners, the interface relies entirely on scale, weight, and negative space to communicate structure. Monospace labels provide technical precision, while serif pull quotes inject warmth and humanity.

Principle 01

Typographic Scale

Extreme contrast between display headings (up to 9rem) and micro-copy (11px). This establishes an unignorable visual hierarchy.

Principle 02

Editorial Layout

Asymmetric grids, hanging punctuation, and generous negative space borrowed directly from high-end print magazines.

Principle 03

Restrained Palette

A monochromatic foundation of near-black and warm white, punctuated exclusively by a high-voltage vermillion accent.

Principle 04

Underline Interactions

Instead of standard button containers, interactive elements rely on precise, scale-transforming underline animations.
