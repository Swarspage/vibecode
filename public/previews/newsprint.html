Section Rules

In modern web design, whitespace is often treated as a luxury—a sign of premium aesthetics. However, the Newsprint system challenges this notion. It argues that high information density, when organized by a rigorous grid and clear typographic hierarchy, is not cluttered, but authoritative.

By studying the layout of broadsheet newspapers, we can extract patterns that have guided readers through massive amounts of information for centuries. Thick rules (4px) separate major sections, while thin rules (1px) divide individual stories.

The use of justified text creates clean blocks of content that align perfectly with the grid, reinforcing the structural integrity of the page. The occasional drop cap anchors the eye, providing a clear entry point into long-form reading.

When interacting with the system, hover states avoid soft fades. Instead, elements exhibit a hard offset shadow—a sudden physical jump that feels deliberate, snappy, and unpolished in the best way possible.
